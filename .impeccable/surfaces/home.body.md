## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Stargaze is a planetarium that programs one cosmic show per day — the visitor sits in dome darkness and today's APOD is the projection; everything else is the evening's program card.

**OWN-WORLD:** Deep dome void (#0a0e1a), warm horizon glow (amber/copper at the bottom curve), seat-velvet accent (#c41e3a), projection white (#f5f0e8). Tenor Sans marquee lettering; Source Sans 3 for reading copy. UI elements feel like embossed program cards and brass ticket stubs — no glassmorphism, no violet sci-fi.

**STORY:** The visitor arrives, sees what's playing tonight (today's APOD filling the dome), reads the show notes, scans supporting headlines on program cards, and can browse the archive of past shows.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Full-height dome interior — APOD image projected with radial vignette and subtle dome arc at the bottom horizon. "Tonight's Show" in tracked caps above the title. Showtime date as brass ticket stub. Two actions: "Past Programs" (gallery) and "Headlines" (news). Horizon glow anchors the bottom.

**FORM:** Planetarium Dome Program (pick, not assigned roll). Seed 20639971.
